## E. 750–800 Challenge

10. **750–800 · SPR.** Student tickets cost `$8`, adult tickets `$12`. At least `$960` revenue is required. If exactly 60 student tickets are sold and at most 100 total tickets may be sold, what is the minimum number of adult tickets?

<details><summary><strong>Practice V2 answer key</strong></summary>

1. **C.** Pattern: inequality with negative coefficient; sign flips only at division by negative.
2. **B.** Strict inequality excludes boundary.
3. **-2.** Expand: `-6x+3≤15 → -6x≤12 → x≥-2`.
4. **A.** Subtract 1, divide by 2 across all three parts.
5. **11.** `120+35w≥500 → w≥10.857...`; context requires whole weeks.
6. **B.** `5=2(2)+1`; strict `>` excludes equality.
7. **A.** `(3,3)` meets both. Boundary reasoning matters.
8. **3.** `ax>6`; for positive a, `x>6/a`; set `6/a=2`.
9. **-3.** `-5x>15 → x<-3`.
10. **40.** Revenue: `480+12a≥960 → a≥40`; total constraint gives `a≤40`, so exactly 40.

</details>
