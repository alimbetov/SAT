# Linear Inequalities — Topic Drill 02

## Questions

1. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `(x-2)/3≤4`.
2. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `5-2x>-7`.
3. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `-3(x+1)≤9`.
4. **Hard · MC.** Solve `4≤2x+2<10`.  
A) `1≤x<4`  B) `1<x≤4`  C) `x≥1`  D) `x<4`
5. **Medium · SPR.** A business earns `$12` per item and has `$40` fixed cost. How many whole items must be sold for profit to be at least `$200`?
6. **Medium · MC.** Does `(3,2)` satisfy `y≤-x+5`?  
A) yes  B) no  C) only if boundary is dashed  D) cannot determine
7. **Hard · MC.** Which point satisfies `x≥2`, `y>1`, and `x+y<6`?  
A) `(2,1)`  B) `(3,2)`  C) `(4,2)`  D) `(1,3)`
8. **Medium · SPR.** What is the greatest integer satisfying `2.5x+1<10`?
9. **Hard · SPR.** What is the minimum `k` such that `x=4` satisfies `3x+k≥20`?
10. **750–800 · SPR.** Scores are `72`, `88`, `94`, and `x`. What minimum integer `x` makes the average strictly greater than 85?

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **x≤14**.
2. **x<6**.
3. **x≥-4**.
4. **A**.
5. **20** — `12n-40≥200`.
6. **A** — equality holds: `2=-3+5`.
7. **B, (3,2)**.
8. **3** — `x<3.6`.
9. **8**.
10. **87** — `254+x>340`.
