# Linear Equations — Topic Drill 02

Уровень: Medium → Hard → 750–800.

## Questions

1. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `x/3+5=9`.
2. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `0.4x+3=7`.
3. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `5(x-2)-3=2(x+4)`.
4. **Hard · SPR.** Solve `(2x-1)/3=(x+5)/2`.
5. **Medium · SPR.** A taxi charges `$8` plus `$2.40` per mile. A ride costs `$32`. How many miles were traveled?
6. **Hard · SPR.** For what `k` does `(k+1)x+2=5x+7` have no solution?
7. **Hard · SPR.** For what `a` does `3(ax-2)=12x-6` have infinitely many solutions?
8. **Hard · SPR.** The equation `(k-3)x+4=10` has solution `x=-2`. Find `k`.
9. **Medium · SPR.** “Three less than twice a number is 17.” Find the number.
10. **750–800 · SPR.** Solve `(x-2)/5+(x+3)/2=7`.

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **12**.
2. **10**.
3. **7**.
4. **17** — cross multiply: `4x-2=3x+15`.
5. **10**.
6. **4** — equal `x` coefficients but unequal constants.
7. **4**.
8. **0**.
9. **10**.
10. **59/7**.

</details>
