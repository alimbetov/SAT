# Systems of Equations — Topic Drill 02

## Questions

1. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `x=2y+1` and `x+y=10`. Find `x`.
2. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `4x+3y=18` and `2x-3y=0`. Find `x`.
3. **Hard · SPR.** For what `k` does `y=kx+2` together with `2y=6x+10` have no solution?
4. **Hard · SPR.** For what `k` do `3x+ky=9` and `6x+8y=18` represent the same line?
5. **Medium · SPR.** Two item types total 12 items. Type A costs `$5`, type B costs `$8`, and total cost is `$81`. How many Type A items are there?
6. **Medium · SPR.** Plans cost `30+4m` and `12+7m`. For what `m` are they equal?
7. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `y=-x+7` and `y=2x-2`. Find `y`.
8. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `3x+y=11` and `x-y=1`. Find `x+y`.
9. **Hard · MC.** For what `k` do `(k-1)x+2y=5` and `4x+4y=10` have infinitely many solutions?  
A) 1  B) 2  C) 3  D) 5
10. **750–800 · SPR.** A theater sold 30 tickets. Adult tickets cost `$14`, child tickets `$8`, and revenue was `$330`. How many adult tickets were sold?

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **7**.
2. **3**.
3. **3** — second line is `y=3x+5`.
4. **4** — divide second equation by 2.
5. **5** — `a+b=12`, `5a+8b=81`.
6. **6**.
7. **4** — intersection `(3,4)`.
8. **5** — solution `(3,2)`.
9. **C, 3**.
10. **15** — `a+c=30`, `14a+8c=330`.

</details>
