# Systems of Equations — Practice V2 · Drill 02

## A. Method Selection

1. **Medium · MC.** For `3x+2y=14` and `5x-2y=10`, which first move is shortest?  
A) add equations  B) solve both for y  C) graph by hand  D) divide everything by x

2. **Medium · MC.** For `y=4x-3` and `2x+y=15`, which method is shortest?  
A) substitution  B) elimination after multiplying both by 100  C) distance formula  D) factoring

## B. Hard Variations

3. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `3x+2y=14`, `5x-2y=10`. Find `x`.

4. **Hard · SPR.** For what `k` do `kx+2y=6` and `3x+2y=6` have infinitely many solutions?

5. **Hard · SPR.** For what `k` do `2x+ky=8` and `x+2y=7` have no solution?

## C. Trap Clinic

6. **Hard · MC.** A student solves only the first equation of a system and stops. What has not been checked?  
A) whether the pair satisfies both equations  B) whether x is positive  C) whether slope exists  D) whether units are metric

7. **Hard · MC.** A system reduces to `0=0`. This means:  
A) no solution  B) exactly one solution  C) infinitely many solutions if equations are equivalent  D) x=0 only

## D. Transfer

8. **Hard · SPR.** Adult tickets cost `$12`, student tickets `$8`. A group buys 30 tickets for `$300`. How many adult tickets were bought?

9. **Hard · SPR.** Plan A costs `25+4m`; Plan B costs `10+7m`. At what `m` are costs equal?

## E. 750–800 Challenge

10. **750–800 · SPR.** The system `kx+2y=10` and `3x-y=4` has a solution with `x=2`. Find `k`.

<details><summary><strong>Practice V2 answer key</strong></summary>

1. **A.** Opposite y-coefficients → elimination.
2. **A.** `y` already isolated.
3. **3.** Add equations: `8x=24`.
4. **3.** Equations must be identical; match x-coefficients.
5. **4.** Slopes equal when `-2/k=-1/2`; constants make distinct lines.
6. **A.** System solution is a pair satisfying both conditions. Error code `K`.
7. **C.** Identity after valid elimination indicates dependent equations.
8. **15.** `a+s=30`, `12a+8s=300`.
9. **5.** Set models equal: `25+4m=10+7m`.
10. **3.** Second equation gives `y=2`; first gives `2k+4=10`.

</details>
