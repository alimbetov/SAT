## E. 750–800 Challenge

10. **750–800 · SPR.** A line is perpendicular to `2x-y=7` and passes through the x-intercept of `3x+y=12`. Find the y-intercept of the new line.

<details><summary><strong>Practice V2 answer key</strong></summary>

1. **B.** Pattern: standard form → slope-intercept form. Method choice matters more than extra work.
2. **1/2.** `-4y=-2x+8 → y=(1/2)x-2`.
3. **1.** Slope `(-12)/4=-3`; use `(−1,4)`: `4=3+b → b=1`.
4. **4.** Slopes: `-k/6=-2/3`.
5. **-2.** Perpendicular slope to `1/3` is `-3`; `a-1=-3`.
6. **B.** First line simplifies to `2x-y=3`, same slope, different intercept.
7. **A.** Pattern: point-slope form.
8. **4.** `Δy/Δx=(38-14)/(8-2)=24/6`.
9. **-1/2.** From `(0,4)` to `(8,0)`.
10. **2.** Given line has slope `2`, so perpendicular slope `-1/2`; x-intercept of `3x+y=12` is `(4,0)`; new line `y=-x/2+2`.

</details>
