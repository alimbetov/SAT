# Linear Equations in Two Variables — Topic Drill 02

## Questions

1. **Medium · SPR.** Find the slope of `5x+2y=10`.
2. **Medium · SPR.** For `4x-3y=12`, find the `x`-intercept and `y`-intercept.
3. **Medium · MC.** Which equation passes through `(-1,2)` and `(3,10)`?  
A) `y=2x+4`  B) `y=2x-4`  C) `y=-2x+4`  D) `y=4x+2`
4. **Medium · SPR.** A line parallel to `y=-3x+5` passes through `(2,0)`. What is its `y`-intercept?
5. **Hard · MC.** A line perpendicular to `2x-y=4` passes through `(4,1)`. Which equation represents it?  
A) `y=-1/2x+3`  B) `y=2x-7`  C) `y=1/2x-1`  D) `y=-2x+9`
6. **Medium · MC.** A line has `x`-intercept 5 and `y`-intercept 10. Which equation represents it?  
A) `x+y=15`  B) `2x+y=10`  C) `x+2y=10`  D) `5x+10y=1`
7. **Hard · SPR.** For what `k` does `kx+3y=12` have slope `2`?
8. **Medium · SPR.** A line has `x`-intercept `-2` and `y`-intercept `4`. Find its slope.
9. **Medium · SPR.** A cost model is `C=75+18t`. For what `t` is `C=237`?
10. **Hard · SPR.** The point `(k,5)` lies on `2x+y=17`. Find `k`.

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **-5/2**.
2. **3 and -4**.
3. **A**.
4. **6** — `0=-3(2)+b`.
5. **A** — original slope 2, perpendicular slope `-1/2`.
6. **B**.
7. **-6** — slope is `-k/3`.
8. **2**.
9. **9**.
10. **6**.

</details>
