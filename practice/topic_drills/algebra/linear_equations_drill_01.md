# Linear Equations — Topic Drill 01

10 оригинальных задач. Решайте без просмотра ответов.

## Questions

1. **Easy · MC.** Solve `4x+3=19`.  
A) 3  B) 4  C) 5  D) 6

2. **Easy · SPR.** Solve `7-2x=15`.

3. **Easy · SPR.** Solve `3(x-2)=21`.

4. **Medium · MC.** Solve `5x+8=2x+29`.  
A) 5  B) 6  C) 7  D) 9

5. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `(x+3)/4=5`.

6. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `2(3x-1)=4x+10`.

7. **Hard · MC.** For what value of `k` does the equation `k(x+2)=4x+9` have no solution?  
A) 2  B) 4  C) `9/2`  D) 9

8. **Hard · SPR.** For what value of `a` does `(a-1)x+6=5x+6` have infinitely many solutions?

9. **Medium · SPR.** A service charges `$22` initially plus `$6` per month. The total bill is `$88`. How many months were charged?

10. **750–800 · SPR.** The equation `(k+1)x-5=13` has solution `x=3`. What is `k`?

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **B, 4** — `4x=16`.
2. **-4** — `-2x=8`.
3. **9** — divide by 3 first: `x-2=7`.
4. **C, 7** — `3x=21`.
5. **17** — `x+3=20`.
6. **6** — `6x-2=4x+10` → `2x=12`.
7. **B, 4** — then `4x+8=4x+9`, impossible.
8. **6** — coefficients must match: `a-1=5`.
9. **11** — `22+6m=88`.
10. **5** — `3(k+1)-5=13` → `3k=15`.

</details>
