# Percentages — Practice V2 · Drill 01

## A. Recognition
1. **Easy · MC.** “30% of 90” means: A) add .30 B) multiply by .30 C) divide by 30 D) multiply by 30
2. **Easy · MC.** Increase by 15% uses multiplier: A) .15 B) .85 C) 1.15 D) 15

## B. Core Pattern
3. **Easy · SPR.** Find 30% of 90.
4. **Easy · SPR.** A `$50` item is discounted 10%. Find sale price.
5. **Medium · SPR.** A value rises from 400 to 460. Find percent increase.

## C. Guided Variation
6. **Medium · SPR.** After a 20% discount, a product costs `$96`. Find original price.
7. **Medium · SPR.** A quantity of 250 increases by 8%. Find new value.

## D. Word Problem
8. **Medium · SPR.** A bill is `$80` before 7.5% tax. Find the tax amount.

## E. Trap
9. **Hard · MC.** Percent change denominator should usually be: A) new value B) old/original value C) difference D) 100

## F. Transfer
10. **Hard · SPR.** A value increases 10% twice. Find total percent increase.

<details><summary><strong>Practice V2 answer key</strong></summary>
1. **B** — “of” → multiply.
2. **C** — `1+.15`.
3. **27** — `90×.30`.
4. **45** — multiply by .90.
5. **15** — change/base=`60/400`.
6. **120** — reverse percent: `96/.8`.
7. **270** — `250×1.08`.
8. **6** — `80×.075`.
9. **B** — base is original value.
10. **21** — `1.1²=1.21`.
</details>
