# Percentages — Topic Drill 01

## Questions

1. **Easy · SPR.** What is 20% of 90?
2. **Easy · SPR.** Increase 80 by 25%.
3. **Easy · SPR.** Decrease 250 by 12%.
4. **Medium · SPR.** A quantity rises from 40 to 50. What is the percent increase?
5. **Medium · SPR.** After a 20% discount, an item costs `$72`. What was the original price?
6. **Easy · SPR.** A `$50` purchase is taxed at 8%. What is the total cost?
7. **Medium · MC.** A value starts at 100, increases by 10%, then decreases by 10%. What is the final value?  
A) 90  B) 99  C) 100  D) 101
8. **Easy · SPR.** 18 is what percent of 24?
9. **Hard · SPR.** After a 15% increase, a value is 230. What was the original value?
10. **Medium · SPR.** A survey result rises from 42% to 51%. By how many **percentage points** did it rise?

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **18**.
2. **100**.
3. **220**.
4. **25%**.
5. **90**.
6. **54**.
7. **B, 99** — `100×1.10×0.90=99`.
8. **75%**.
9. **200** — `1.15x=230`.
10. **9 percentage points**.

</details>
