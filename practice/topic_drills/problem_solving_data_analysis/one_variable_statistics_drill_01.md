# One-Variable Statistics — Topic Drill 01

## Questions

1. **Easy · SPR.** Find the mean of `6, 8, 10, 12`.
2. **Easy · SPR.** Find the median of `2, 5, 7, 11, 20`.
3. **Easy · SPR.** Find the range of `4, 9, 12, 15`.
4. **Medium · SPR.** For `1,3,5,7,9,11,13,15`, find the interquartile range.
5. **Medium · MC.** Which measure is generally more resistant to an extreme outlier?  
A) Mean  B) Median  C) Range  D) Standard deviation
6. **Medium · SPR.** A group of 10 students has mean 72, and a group of 15 students has mean 80. What is the combined mean?
7. **Medium · MC.** Which data set has larger standard deviation?  
A) `9,10,11`  B) `0,10,20`  C) equal  D) cannot determine
8. **Easy · MC.** In a box plot, the line inside the box usually represents the  
A) mean  B) median  C) maximum  D) standard deviation
9. **Hard · MC.** If 5 is added to every value in a data set, what happens?  
A) Mean +5 and SD +5  
B) Mean +5 and SD unchanged  
C) Mean unchanged and SD +5  
D) Both unchanged
10. **Hard · SPR.** Five numbers have mean 10. A sixth number, 16, is added. What is the new mean?

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **9**.
2. **7**.
3. **11**.
4. **8** — `Q1=4`, `Q3=12`.
5. **B, median**.
6. **76.8** — `(10·72+15·80)/25=1920/25`.
7. **B**.
8. **B**.
9. **B** — shifting all values changes center, not spread.
10. **11** — original sum 50; new sum 66 over 6 values.
