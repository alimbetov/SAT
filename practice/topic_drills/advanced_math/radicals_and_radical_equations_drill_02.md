# Radicals and Radical Equations — Topic Drill 02

## Questions

1. **Medium · MC.** Simplify `√200`.  
A) `5√8`  B) `10√2`  C) `20√2`  D) `100√2`
2. **Easy · SPR.** Evaluate `∛(-64)`.
3. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `√(3x+4)=7`.
4. **Hard · SPR.** Solve `√(x-1)=x-7`.
5. **Medium · MC.** Rationalize `1/√2`.  
A) `√2`  B) `√2/2`  C) `2√2`  D) `1/2`
6. **Medium · SPR.** Evaluate `8^(2/3)`.
7. **Medium · MC.** What is the domain of `√(5-2x)`?  
A) `x≥5/2`  B) `x≤5/2`  C) `x≠5/2`  D) all real numbers
8. **750–800 · SPR.** Solve `√(x+9)-√x=1`.
9. **Hard · SPR.** The equation `√(x+k)=x` has solution `x=4`. Find `k`.
10. **Hard · SPR.** Solve `√(2x+8)=x`.

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **B**.
2. **-4**.
3. **15**.
4. **10** — squaring gives 5 and 10; only 10 satisfies the original equation.
5. **B**.
6. **4**.
7. **B**.
8. **16**.
9. **12**.
10. **4** — candidate `-2` is invalid because the square root cannot equal a negative number.

</details>
