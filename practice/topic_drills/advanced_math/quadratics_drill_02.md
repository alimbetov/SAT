# Quadratics — Topic Drill 02

## Questions

1. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `2x^2-8x=0`. What is the larger solution?
2. **Medium · MC.** What is the vertex of `y=x^2-10x+21`?  
A) `(5,-4)`  B) `(-5,-4)`  C) `(5,4)`  D) `(10,21)`
3. **Medium · SPR.** What is the minimum value of `y=3(x+2)^2-7`?
4. **Medium · SPR.** Find the sum of the roots of `x^2+2x-8=0`.
5. **Hard · MC.** For which values of `k` does `3x^2+kx+12=0` have exactly one real solution?  
A) `k=12` only  B) `k=-12` only  C) `k=±12`  D) `k=±6`
6. **Hard · SPR.** If `x=3` is a root of `x^2+kx-18=0`, find `k`.
7. **Hard · SPR.** A rectangle has width `x`, length `x+5`, and area 84. Find the positive width.
8. **Medium · SPR.** A height function is `h(t)=-16t^2+64t+5`. At what time is maximum height reached?
9. **Hard · SPR.** A parabola has axis of symmetry `x=4` and one root at `x=1`. What is the other root?
10. **750–800 · MC.** A quadratic has roots 2 and 6 and passes through `(0,12)`. Which equation represents it?  
A) `y=x^2-8x+12`  B) `y=2x^2-16x+24`  C) `y=x^2+8x+12`  D) `y=(x-2)(x+6)`

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **4**.
2. **A** — complete square to `(x-5)^2-4`.
3. **-7**.
4. **-2**.
5. **C** — `k^2-144=0`.
6. **3**.
7. **7** — `x^2+5x-84=0`.
8. **2**.
9. **7** — roots are symmetric around 4.
10. **A** — `a(x-2)(x-6)` and `12=12a`, so `a=1`.

</details>
