# Quadratics — Practice V2 · Drill 02

## A. Method Selection
1. **Medium · MC.** Fastest method for `x²=49`? A) quadratic formula B) square root C) graph only D) complete square
2. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `x²-10x+25=0`.

## B. Hard Variations
3. **Hard · SPR.** For what `k` does `x²+6x+k=0` have exactly one real root?
4. **Hard · SPR.** Find the minimum value of `y=2x²-8x+3`.
5. **Hard · SPR.** Rewrite `x²-6x+13=(x-3)²+k`. Find `k`.

## C. Trap Clinic
6. **Medium · MC.** Vertex of `y=(x+4)²-7`? A) `(4,-7)` B) `(-4,-7)` C) `(-4,7)` D) `(4,7)`
7. **Hard · SPR.** Graphs `y=x²` and `y=6x-5` intersect at two x-values. Find the larger one.

## D. Transfer
8. **Hard · SPR.** A monic quadratic has roots 3 and 7. Find the coefficient of x.
9. **Hard · SPR.** A rectangle has side lengths `x` and `x+3` and area 40. Find positive `x`.

## E. 750–800 Challenge
10. **750–800 · SPR.** The horizontal line `y=k` touches `y=x²-6x+13` at exactly one point. Find `k`.

<details><summary><strong>Practice V2 answer key</strong></summary>
1. **B** — `x=±7`; simplest structure wins.
2. **5** — perfect square `(x-5)²`.
3. **9** — discriminant zero.
4. **-5** — vertex x=2, then evaluate.
5. **4** — complete square.
6. **B** — inside sign reverses.
7. **5** — factor `x²-6x+5`.
8. **-10** — `(x-3)(x-7)=x²-10x+21`.
9. **5** — `x²+3x-40=0`.
10. **4** — minimum/vertex value.
</details>
