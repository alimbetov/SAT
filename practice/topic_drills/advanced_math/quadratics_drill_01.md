# Quadratics — Topic Drill 01

## Questions

1. **Easy · SPR.** What is the larger root of `x^2-7x+12=0`?
2. **Easy · MC.** What is the vertex of `y=(x-2)^2+5`?  
A) `(-2,5)`  B) `(2,5)`  C) `(2,-5)`  D) `(-2,-5)`
3. **Medium · SPR.** What is the axis of symmetry of `y=x^2+6x+1`?
4. **Medium · MC.** How many real solutions does `x^2-4x+7=0` have?  
A) 0  B) 1  C) 2  D) infinitely many
5. **Hard · SPR.** For what `k` does `x^2+6x+k=0` have exactly one real root?
6. **Medium · SPR.** What is the maximum value of `y=-2(x+1)^2+8`?
7. **Easy · MC.** The roots of `x^2-9x+20=0` are 4 and 5. What is their product?  
A) 9  B) 20  C) 45  D) 1
8. **Hard · SPR.** A height model is `h(t)=-5t^2+20t+1`. At what time `t` is the maximum height reached?
9. **Medium · MC.** Which quadratic has roots 2 and 5?  
A) `x^2-7x+10`  B) `x^2+7x+10`  C) `x^2-3x+10`  D) `x^2-10x+7`
10. **750–800 · SPR.** A quadratic has vertex `(3,-4)` and form `y=a(x-3)^2-4`. It passes through `(1,4)`. Find `a`.

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **4**.
2. **B**.
3. **-3** — `x=-b/(2a)`.
4. **A** — discriminant `16-28<0`.
5. **9** — `36-4k=0`.
6. **8**.
7. **B, 20**.
8. **2** — vertex time `-20/(2·-5)`.
9. **A**.
10. **2** — `4=4a-4`.

</details>
