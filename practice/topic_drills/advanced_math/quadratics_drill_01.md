# Quadratics — Practice V2 · Drill 01

## A. Recognition
1. **Easy · MC.** Which form shows roots most directly? A) `ax²+bx+c` B) `a(x-r1)(x-r2)` C) `a(x-h)²+k` D) table
2. **Easy · MC.** What is the vertex of `y=(x-2)²+5`? A) `(-2,5)` B) `(2,5)` C) `(2,-5)` D) `(-2,-5)`

## B. Core Pattern
3. **Easy · SPR.** Find the larger root of `x²-7x+12=0`.
4. **Medium · SPR.** Find the axis of symmetry of `y=x²+6x+1`.
5. **Medium · MC.** How many real solutions does `x²-4x+7=0` have? A) 0 B) 1 C) 2 D) infinitely many

## C. Guided Variation
6. **Medium · SPR.** What is the maximum value of `y=-2(x+1)²+8`?
7. **Easy · SPR.** The roots of `x²-9x+20=0` are 4 and 5. Find their product.

## D. Word Problem
8. **Hard · SPR.** `h(t)=-5t²+20t+1`. At what time is maximum height reached?

## E. Trap
9. **Medium · MC.** Solve `x²=25`. A) 5 only B) -5 only C) ±5 D) 25

## F. Transfer
10. **Hard · SPR.** `f(x)=a(x-2)(x-8)` passes through `(0,16)`. Find `a`.

<details><summary><strong>Practice V2 answer key</strong></summary>
1. **B** — factored form → roots.
2. **B** — vertex form; inside sign reverses.
3. **4** — factor `(x-3)(x-4)`.
4. **-3** — `-b/(2a)`.
5. **A** — discriminant `16-28<0`.
6. **8** — downward parabola; vertex gives maximum.
7. **20** — direct root product.
8. **2** — vertex time `-b/(2a)`.
9. **C** — trap: remember `±`.
10. **1** — substitute `(0,16)`.
