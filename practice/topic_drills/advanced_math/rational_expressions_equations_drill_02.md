<details><summary><strong>Practice V2 answer key</strong></summary>

2. **2** — multiply by 2x; check x≠0.
3. **-5** — cross multiply; valid.
4. **6** — clear denominator.
5. **1/2** — x factors cancel, restriction x≠0 remains.
6. **C** — removable hole is still undefined in original expression.
7. **C** — denominator zero at x=k.
8. **4/3** — `x=4x-4`.
9. **2** — x=2,3.
10. **5** — simplify to x+3=8; x=3 excluded but not candidate.
