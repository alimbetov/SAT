# Rational Expressions and Equations — Topic Drill 02

## Questions

1. **Medium · MC.** For `x≠0`, simplify `(x^2-5x)/x`.  
A) `x-5`  B) `x+5`  C) `x^2-5`  D) `1-5x`
2. **Hard · SPR.** Solve `4/(x-1)=2/(x+2)`.
3. **Medium · MC.** Simplify `1/x+1/(x+1)`.  
A) `(2x+1)/(x(x+1))`  B) `2/(2x+1)`  C) `(x+1)/x`  D) `1/(x^2+x)`
4. **750–800 · SPR.** Solve `1/(x-2)+1/(x+2)=1`. What is the larger solution?
5. **Medium · MC.** For `y=(2x+1)/(x-3)`, the vertical and horizontal asymptotes are  
A) `x=3`, `y=2`  B) `x=2`, `y=3`  C) `x=-3`, `y=-2`  D) `x=1`, `y=2`
6. **Hard · MC.** The graph of `(x^2-1)/(x-1)` has a hole at  
A) `(1,0)`  B) `(1,2)`  C) `(-1,0)`  D) `(0,1)`
7. **Hard · SPR.** `(x+k)/(x-1)=3` has solution `x=4`. Find `k`.
8. **Medium · MC.** Which values are excluded from `(x+1)/((x-2)(x+5))`?  
A) 2 only  B) -5 only  C) 2 and -5  D) -1 and 1
9. **Hard · SPR.** Solve `(x+3)/(x-2)=1/2`.
10. **750–800 · MC.** How many real solutions does `(x^2-4)/(x+2)=x-2` have?  
A) 0  B) 1  C) 2  D) infinitely many, except the excluded value

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **A**.
2. **-5** — cross multiply: `4x+8=2x-2`.
3. **A**.
4. **`1+√5`** — equation becomes `x^2-2x-4=0`.
5. **A**.
6. **B** — simplified function is `y=x+1`, but `x=1` is excluded.
7. **5**.
8. **C**.
9. **-8** — `2x+6=x-2`.
10. **D** — identity holds for every real `x` except `x=-2`.

</details>
