# Nonlinear Equations and Systems — Topic Drill 02

## Questions

1. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `x^2-25=0`. What is the positive solution?
2. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `x^3=27`.
3. **Medium · MC.** How many real solutions does `x^4=16` have?  
A) 0  B) 1  C) 2  D) 4
4. **Medium · MC.** How many intersections do `y=x^2+1` and `y=5` have?  
A) 0  B) 1  C) 2  D) 4
5. **Hard · SPR.** The graphs `y=x^2` and `y=-2x+3` intersect at two points. What is the sum of their `x`-coordinates?
6. **Medium · MC.** How many points satisfy `x^2+y^2=25` and `x=3`?  
A) 0  B) 1  C) 2  D) 5
7. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `x^2+y=7`, `y=3`. What is the positive `x`?
8. **Hard · SPR.** For what `k` do `y=x^2` and `y=k` have exactly one real intersection?
9. **Hard · SPR.** Solve `√(x+6)=x`.
10. **750–800 · MC.** For real `m`, how many intersections do `y=x^2-4` and `y=mx` always have?  
A) 0  B) 1  C) 2  D) depends on `m`

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **5**.
2. **3**.
3. **C, 2** — `x=±2`.
4. **C, 2**.
5. **-2** — roots of `x^2+2x-3=0` sum to -2.
6. **C, 2** — `y=±4`.
7. **2**.
8. **0** — `x^2=0` has one real solution.
9. **3** — candidate -2 is invalid.
10. **C, 2** — `x^2-mx-4=0` has discriminant `m^2+16>0` for all real `m`.

</details>
