# Nonlinear Equations & Systems — Practice V2 · Drill 02

## A. Method Selection
1. **Medium · MC.** For an ugly nonlinear system with both equations easily graphed, fastest valid SAT tool may be: A) Desmos intersections B) long division C) unit conversion D) mean
2. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `x²=2x+8`. Enter the larger solution.

## B. Hard Variations
3. **Hard · SPR.** Line `y=2x+k` is tangent to `y=x²`. Find `k`.
4. **Hard · SPR.** Solve `√(x+6)=x`.
5. **Hard · SPR.** Solve `1/x=2/(x+3)`.

## C. Trap Clinic
6. **Medium · MC.** After solving a radical equation by squaring, final step? A) graph only B) check candidates in original C) round all answers D) add roots
7. **Medium · MC.** How many real intersections do `y=x²` and `y=-1` have? A)0 B)1 C)2 D)∞

## D. Transfer
8. **Hard · SPR.** Systems `y=x+2` and `y=x²-4` have two intersections. Find the sum of their y-coordinates.
9. **Hard · SPR.** `y=x²` and `y=4x+k` intersect exactly once. Find `k`.

## E. 750–800 Challenge
10. **750–800 · SPR.** `y=x²-6x+13` and `y=k` intersect exactly once. Find `k`.
