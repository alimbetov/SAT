# Equivalent Expressions — Practice V2 · Drill 01

## A. Recognition
1. **Easy · MC.** `x²-16` is which pattern? A) perfect square B) difference of squares C) linear D) rational
2. **Easy · MC.** Which form is usually best for reading zeros? A) expanded B) factored C) decimal D) table only

## B. Core Pattern
3. **Easy · SPR.** Simplify `4(x+3)-2x`. Enter the constant term.
4. **Easy · SPR.** In `6x+12=6(x+k)`, find `k`.
5. **Easy · MC.** Factor `x²-9`. A) `(x-3)²` B) `(x-3)(x+3)` C) `(x-9)(x+1)` D) prime

## C. Guided Variation
6. **Medium · SPR.** If `x²+10x+25=(x+a)²`, find `a`.
7. **Medium · SPR.** Simplify `x²·x³` to `x^n`. Find `n`.

## D. Word/Structure
8. **Medium · MC.** A rectangle has sides `x+2` and `x+5`. Which expression is its area? A) `x²+7x+10` B) `2x+7` C) `x²+10` D) `x²+3x+10`

## E. Trap
9. **Hard · MC.** Which is correct? A) `(a+b)²=a²+b²` B) `(a+b)²=a²+2ab+b²` C) `(a+b)²=2a²+2b²` D) `(a+b)²=a²+ab+b²`

## F. Transfer
10. **Hard · SPR.** `(x+a)²=x²+14x+49`. Find positive `a`.
