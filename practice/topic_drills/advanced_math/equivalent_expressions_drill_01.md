# Equivalent Expressions — Topic Drill 01

## Questions

1. **Easy · MC.** Simplify `3x+2x-4`.  
A) `5x-4`  B) `5x+4`  C) `6x-4`  D) `x-4`
2. **Easy · SPR.** Expand `4(2x-3)`.
3. **Easy · MC.** Factor `6x+18`.  
A) `6(x+3)`  B) `3(2x+18)`  C) `6(x+18)`  D) `2(3x+18)`
4. **Medium · MC.** Factor `x^2+5x+6`.  
A) `(x+1)(x+6)`  B) `(x+2)(x+3)`  C) `(x-2)(x-3)`  D) `(x+5)(x+1)`
5. **Easy · MC.** Factor `x^2-16`.  
A) `(x-8)(x+2)`  B) `(x-4)^2`  C) `(x-4)(x+4)`  D) `(x-16)(x+1)`
6. **Medium · MC.** For `x≠3`, simplify `(x^2-9)/(x-3)`.  
A) `x-3`  B) `x+3`  C) `x^2+3`  D) `1`
7. **Medium · SPR.** Factor completely: `2x^2+8x`.
8. **Easy · SPR.** Simplify `x^3·x^5`.
9. **Medium · SPR.** Simplify `(2x^2y)(3xy^3)`.
10. **Hard · SPR.** If `a(x+4)` is equivalent to `3x+12` for all `x`, what is `a`?

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **A**.
2. **`8x-12`**.
3. **A**.
4. **B**.
5. **C**.
6. **B** — cancel factor `(x-3)` while keeping `x≠3`.
7. **`2x(x+4)`**.
8. **`x^8`**.
9. **`6x^3y^4`**.
10. **3**.

</details>
