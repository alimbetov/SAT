# Exponential Functions — Topic Drill 01

## Questions

1. **Easy · MC.** A population starts at 200 and grows by 5% per year. Which model is correct?  
A) `200(1.05)^t`  B) `200(0.95)^t`  C) `205t`  D) `200+5t`
2. **Easy · MC.** A value starts at 800 and decreases by 10% per year. Which model is correct?  
A) `800(1.1)^t`  B) `800(0.9)^t`  C) `800-10t`  D) `80^t`
3. **Easy · SPR.** What growth factor corresponds to a 12% increase?
4. **Easy · SPR.** What decay factor corresponds to a 35% decrease?
5. **Easy · SPR.** Solve `2^x=32`.
6. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `3^(x-1)=27`.
7. **Medium · MC.** A culture doubles every 3 hours and begins at 100. Which model is correct?  
A) `100·2^(t/3)`  B) `100·3^(t/2)`  C) `100+2t`  D) `200^t`
8. **Hard · SPR.** A quantity grows from 500 to 605 in 2 years at a constant annual percentage rate. What is the annual percent increase?
9. **Medium · MC.** Which is equivalent to `5·4^(x+1)`?  
A) `20·4^x`  B) `5·4^x+1`  C) `20^x`  D) `5·5^x`
10. **Hard · SPR.** An exponential function has form `f(x)=ab^x`, with `f(0)=12` and `f(1)=18`. Find `b`.

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **A**.
2. **B**.
3. **1.12**.
4. **0.65**.
5. **5**.
6. **4** — `27=3^3`.
7. **A**.
8. **10%** — `(1+r)^2=605/500=1.21`, so `1+r=1.1`.
9. **A**.
10. **1.5** — `a=12`, so `12b=18`.

</details>
