# Exponential Functions — Practice V2 · Drill 01

## A. Recognition
1. **Easy · MC.** A quantity increases by 6% each year. Growth factor? A) .06 B) .94 C) 1.06 D) 1.6
2. **Easy · MC.** A quantity decreases by 20% each period. Factor? A) .2 B) .8 C) 1.2 D) 20

## B. Core Pattern
3. **Easy · SPR.** For `P(t)=300(1.08)^t`, find `P(0)`.
4. **Easy · SPR.** What percent growth rate is represented by factor `1.08`?
5. **Medium · MC.** Which is exponential? A) +5 liters/min B) +$20/hour C) loses 15% value/year D) $4 + $2/mile

## C. Guided Variation
6. **Easy · SPR.** `$1000` grows by 10% for one period. Find the new value.
7. **Medium · SPR.** 800 bacteria grow by 25% each hour. Find the population after 2 hours.

## D. Decay
8. **Medium · SPR.** Find `500(0.9)^2`.

## E. Trap
9. **Medium · MC.** Factor `1.12` means: A) +112% B) +12% C) -12% D) +1.2%

## F. Transfer
10. **Hard · SPR.** `P(t)=1200(1.5)^(t/3)`. By what percent does P increase every 3 time units?
