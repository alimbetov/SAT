## B. Hard Variations

5. **Hard · SPR.** A monic cubic has roots `1,4,-2`. Find the coefficient of `x²`.

## C. Trap Clinic

7. **Medium · SPR.** What is the degree of the product of a degree-2 and degree-3 polynomial?

## D. Transfer
8. **Hard · SPR.** `f(x)=(x-1)²(x+3)`. How many distinct real roots are shown?
9. **Hard · SPR.** The remainder when `f(x)` is divided by `(x-1)` is 7. Find `f(1)`.

## E. 750–800 Challenge
10. **750–800 · SPR.** `p(x)=x³+ax²+bx+6` has roots `-1,-2,-3`. Find `a+b`.

<details><summary><strong>Practice V2 answer key</strong></summary>
1. **B** — factor theorem.
2. **1** — `f(2)=4+2k-6=0`.
3. **55** — remainder theorem: `f(3)`.
4. **30** — constant from `(x+2)(x-3)(x-5)`.
5. **-3** — coefficient is negative sum of roots.
6. **B** — sign trap.
7. **5** — degrees add in product.
8. **2** — roots 1 and -3; multiplicity doesn't create new distinct root.
9. **7** — remainder theorem.
