# Polynomial Functions — Topic Drill 02

## Questions

1. **Medium · MC.** For `P(x)=-2x^5+3x^2-1`, what are the degree and leading coefficient?  
A) degree 5, coefficient `-2`  B) degree 2, coefficient 3  C) degree 5, coefficient 3  D) degree 2, coefficient `-2`
2. **Medium · SPR.** Evaluate `P(-2)` for `P(x)=x^3+3x^2-4`.
3. **Hard · MC.** Factor `x^3+2x^2-x-2`.  
A) `(x+2)(x-1)(x+1)`  B) `(x-2)(x-1)(x+1)`  C) `x(x+2)(x-1)`  D) `(x+2)^2(x-1)`
4. **Medium · SPR.** What is the remainder when `x^4-1` is divided by `(x-1)`?
5. **Hard · SPR.** If `(x+2)` is a factor of `x^2+kx-6`, find `k`.
6. **Medium · MC.** A zero has even multiplicity. Near that zero, the graph typically  
A) crosses the x-axis  B) touches and turns  C) has a vertical asymptote  D) becomes linear
7. **Medium · MC.** A polynomial has even degree and positive leading coefficient. Its end behavior is  
A) both ends up  B) both ends down  C) left down/right up  D) left up/right down
8. **Medium · MC.** Which monic quadratic has roots `-2` and `3`?  
A) `x^2-x-6`  B) `x^2+x-6`  C) `x^2-5x+6`  D) `x^2+5x+6`
9. **Hard · SPR.** `P(x)=(x-1)(x+2)(x-4)`. Find `P(0)`.
10. **750–800 · SPR.** A monic cubic has roots 1, 2, and 5. What is the coefficient of `x^2`?

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **A**.
2. **0**.
3. **A**.
4. **0**.
5. **-1**.
6. **B**.
7. **A**.
8. **A**.
9. **8**.
10. **-8** — coefficient is negative sum of roots.
