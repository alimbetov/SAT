# Rational Expressions & Equations — Practice V2 · Drill 01

## A. Recognition
1. **Easy · SPR.** For what x is `5/(x-7)` undefined?
2. **Easy · SPR.** List the positive excluded value for `(x+1)/(x²-9)`.

## B. Core Pattern
3. **Easy · SPR.** Simplify `(x²-16)/(x-4)` for `x≠4`. Enter the constant added to x.
4. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `2/x=6/9`.
5. **Medium · MC.** LCD of `1/x` and `1/(x+2)`? A) `2x` B) `x+2` C) `x(x+2)` D) `x²+2`

## C. Guided Variation
6. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `(x+1)/(x-2)=2`.
7. **Medium · MC.** Which can be canceled? A) terms across `x+2` B) common factors C) anything equal-looking D) additions

## D. Equation
8. **Hard · SPR.** Solve `3/(x-1)=6/(x+2)`.

## E. Trap
9. **Hard · MC.** After simplifying `(x²-25)/(x-5)` to `x+5`, is x=5 allowed? A) yes B) no

## F. Transfer
10. **Hard · SPR.** `(x²-9)/(x²-x-6)` simplifies, but one positive x remains excluded. Find it.
