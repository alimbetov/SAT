# Rational Expressions and Equations — Topic Drill 01

## Questions

1. **Easy · MC.** Which value is excluded from the domain of `1/(x-5)`?  
A) `-5`  B) `0`  C) `5`  D) `1`
2. **Easy · MC.** For `x≠2`, simplify `(x^2-4)/(x-2)`.  
A) `x-2`  B) `x+2`  C) `x^2+2`  D) `1`
3. **Easy · SPR.** Solve `1/x=1/4`.
4. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `2/(x+1)=1`.
5. **Hard · SPR.** Solve `3/(x-2)=x`. What is the sum of all real solutions?
6. **Medium · MC.** Simplify `1/x + 1/(2x)`.  
A) `1/(3x)`  B) `2/(3x)`  C) `3/(2x)`  D) `3x/2`
7. **Medium · SPR.** Solve `(x+2)/(x-1)=3`.
8. **Medium · MC.** For `y=1/(x-3)+2`, which asymptotes are correct?  
A) `x=2`, `y=3`  B) `x=3`, `y=2`  C) `x=-3`, `y=-2`  D) `x=1`, `y=2`
9. **Hard · MC.** Solve `(x^2-9)/(x-3)=6`, with original restriction `x≠3`.  
A) `x=3`  B) `x=-3`  C) no solution  D) infinitely many
10. **750–800 · SPR.** The equation `(x+k)/(x-2)=2` has solution `x=5`. Find `k`.

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **C, 5**.
2. **B**.
3. **4**.
4. **1** — `2=x+1`.
5. **2** — equation gives `x^2-2x-3=0`, roots 3 and -1, sum 2.
6. **C**.
7. **5/2**.
8. **B**.
9. **C** — simplification gives candidate `x=3`, but it is excluded.
10. **1** — `(5+k)/3=2`.

</details>
