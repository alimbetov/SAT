## B. Hard Variations

## C. Trap Clinic

## D. Transfer
8. **Medium · MC.** Constant percentage change suggests: A) linear B) exponential C) absolute D) radical
9. **Hard · SPR.** If point `(1,3)` lies on `y=f(x)`, what is the y-coordinate of the corresponding point after transformation `g(x)=f(x-2)+5`?

## E. 750–800 Challenge
10. **750–800 · SPR.** `y=a|x-3|+2` passes through `(5,10)`. Find `a`.

<details><summary><strong>Practice V2 answer key</strong></summary>
1. **B** — multiplicative pattern.
2. **A** — one turning point/U-shape.
3. **5** — denominator restriction.
4. **B** — inside left3, outside down2.
5. **4** — vertex minimum.
6. **B** — opens downward.
7. **1** — `f(0)=a`.
8. **B** — exponential signature.
9. **8** — vertical shift +5; point moves to (3,8).
10. **4** — `10=2a+2`.
