# Nonlinear Functions — Topic Drill 02

## Questions

1. **Medium · MC.** Which function is symmetric about the `y`-axis?  
A) `x^2`  B) `x^3`  C) `2^x`  D) `1/x`
2. **Medium · MC.** Which statement is true for `f(x)=x^3`?  
A) `f(-x)=f(x)`  B) `f(-x)=-f(x)`  C) `f(x)>0` always  D) domain is `x≥0`
3. **Hard · MC.** What is the domain of `f(x)=√(9-x^2)`?  
A) `x≥3`  B) `x≤-3`  C) `-3≤x≤3`  D) all real numbers
4. **Medium · MC.** What is the range of `y=-(x-2)^2+5`?  
A) `y≥5`  B) `y≤5`  C) `y≥2`  D) all real values
5. **Medium · MC.** Relative to `y=x^2`, `y=2(x-1)^2` is  
A) shifted left 1 and compressed  B) shifted right 1 and vertically stretched  C) shifted up 2  D) reflected over x-axis
6. **Medium · MC.** For `y=1/(x+2)-3`, the asymptotes are  
A) `x=-2`, `y=-3`  B) `x=2`, `y=3`  C) `x=-3`, `y=-2`  D) `x=0`, `y=-3`
7. **Easy · MC.** `y=5(0.8)^x` is  
A) increasing exponential  B) decreasing exponential  C) quadratic  D) linear
8. **Medium · SPR.** Find the minimum value of `y=2|x-4|+3`.
9. **Hard · SPR.** `q(x)=a(x+1)^2-2` and `q(1)=6`. Find `a`.
10. **750–800 · SPR.** A quadratic has zeros `-1` and `5` and value `-5` at `x=0`. In `q(x)=a(x+1)(x-5)`, find `a`.

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **A**.
2. **B**.
3. **C**.
4. **B**.
5. **B**.
6. **A**.
7. **B**.
8. **3**.
9. **2**.
10. **1**.
