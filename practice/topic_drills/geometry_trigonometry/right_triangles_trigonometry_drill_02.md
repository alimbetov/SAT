# Right Triangles and Trigonometry — Topic Drill 02

## Questions

1. **Medium · SPR.** A right triangle has legs 7 and 24. Find the hypotenuse.
2. **Medium · SPR.** A right triangle has hypotenuse 13 and one leg 5. Find the other leg.
3. **Hard · MC.** A `45°-45°-90°` triangle has hypotenuse 10. What is each leg?  
A) `5`  B) `5√2`  C) `10√2`  D) `20`
4. **Medium · MC.** A `30°-60°-90°` triangle has hypotenuse 18. What is the longer leg?  
A) `9`  B) `9√2`  C) `9√3`  D) `18√3`
5. **Medium · SPR.** In a right triangle, opposite = 5 and hypotenuse = 13. Find `sin θ`.
6. **Medium · SPR.** In a right triangle, adjacent = 12 and hypotenuse = 13. Find `cos θ`.
7. **Medium · SPR.** In a right triangle, opposite = 5 and adjacent = 12. Find `tan θ`.
8. **Medium · MC.** Which equals `sin θ` for acute `θ`?  
A) `cos(90°-θ)`  B) `cos θ`  C) `tan θ`  D) `sin(90°-θ)`
9. **Hard · SPR.** A 12-foot pole casts a 5-foot shadow. For the angle of elevation `θ`, find `tan θ`.
10. **750–800 · SPR.** If `tan θ=3/4` for acute `θ`, find `cos θ`.

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **25**.
2. **12**.
3. **B, 5√2**.
4. **C, 9√3**.
5. **5/13**.
6. **12/13**.
7. **5/12**.
8. **A**.
9. **12/5**.
10. **4/5** — use a 3-4-5 triangle.

</details>
