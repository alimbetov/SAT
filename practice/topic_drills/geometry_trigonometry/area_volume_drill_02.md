# Area, Surface Area, and Volume — Topic Drill 02

## Questions

1. **Medium · SPR.** A rectangle is 8 by 13. Find its perimeter.
2. **Medium · SPR.** A trapezoid has bases 7 and 15 and height 6. Find its area.
3. **Medium · SPR.** A parallelogram has base 14 and perpendicular height 9. Find its area.
4. **Hard · MC.** A cylinder has radius 3 and height 5. What is its total surface area?  
A) `30π`  B) `39π`  C) `48π`  D) `60π`
5. **Hard · SPR.** A rectangular prism has dimensions 4, 5, and 6. Find its total surface area.
6. **Medium · MC.** A cylinder has radius 4 and height 10. What is its volume?  
A) `40π`  B) `80π`  C) `160π`  D) `320π`
7. **Hard · SPR.** Similar figures have linear scale factor `1.5`. What is the area scale factor?
8. **Hard · SPR.** Similar solids have linear scale factor `1/2`. What is the volume scale factor?
9. **Medium · SPR.** A cube has volume 343. Find its side length.
10. **750–800 · SPR.** Two similar solids have volume ratio `64:125`. A corresponding length in the smaller solid is 12. Find the corresponding length in the larger solid.

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **42**.
2. **66**.
3. **126**.
4. **C, 48π** — `2πr^2+2πrh`.
5. **148** — `2(4·5+4·6+5·6)`.
6. **C, 160π**.
7. **2.25**.
8. **1/8 = 0.125**.
9. **7**.
10. **15** — linear ratio is cube root of `64:125`, or `4:5`.

</details>
