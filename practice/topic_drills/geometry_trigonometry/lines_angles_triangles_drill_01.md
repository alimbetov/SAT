# Lines, Angles, and Triangles — Topic Drill 01

## Questions

1. **Easy · SPR.** An angle is supplementary to `120°`. What is its measure?
2. **Easy · SPR.** Two vertical angles are formed. One measures `47°`. What is the other?
3. **Easy · MC.** When two parallel lines are cut by a transversal, alternate interior angles are  
A) supplementary  B) congruent  C) always 90°  D) unrelated
4. **Easy · SPR.** A triangle has angles `35°` and `65°`. Find the third angle.
5. **Medium · SPR.** The two remote interior angles of a triangle are `50°` and `60°`. Find the corresponding exterior angle.
6. **Medium · SPR.** An isosceles triangle has vertex angle `40°`. What is each base angle?
7. **Medium · SPR.** Two triangles are similar. A side of length 6 corresponds to a side of length 9. If another side of the smaller triangle is 8, what is the corresponding side of the larger triangle?
8. **Easy · MC.** Which condition guarantees triangle congruence?  
A) AAA  B) SSS  C) same perimeter only  D) same area only
9. **Medium · MC.** Two sides of a triangle are 4 and 7. Which could be the third side?  
A) 2  B) 3  C) 5  D) 12
10. **Hard · SPR.** The angles of a triangle are `x`, `2x`, and `3x` degrees. What is the largest angle?

<details><summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **60°**.
2. **47°**.
3. **B**.
4. **80°**.
5. **110°**.
6. **70°**.
7. **12** — scale factor `9/6=3/2`.
8. **B**.
9. **C, 5** — triangle inequality requires `3<x<11`.
10. **90°** — `6x=180`, so `x=30`.

</details>
