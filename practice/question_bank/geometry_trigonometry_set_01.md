### 17. Medium · SPR · 30-60-90 triangle · Manual
In a `30°-60°-90°` triangle, the shortest side has length `5`. What is the hypotenuse?

### 18. Easy · SPR · Sine · Manual
In a right triangle, relative to angle `θ`, the opposite side has length `6` and the hypotenuse has length `10`. What is `sin θ`? Enter a fraction or decimal.

### 19. Medium · Multiple Choice · Complementary trig · Manual
Which expression is equal to `sin(35°)`?

A) `cos(35°)`  B) `cos(55°)`  C) `sin(55°)`  D) `tan(55°)`

### 20. Medium · Multiple Choice · Degrees/radians · Manual
Which radian measure is equivalent to `150°`?

A) `π/6`  B) `π/3`  C) `5π/6`  D) `3π/2`

<details>
<summary><strong>Answers and brief solutions</strong></summary>

1. **84**. `12×7`.
2. **28**. `2(5+9)`.
3. **35**. `1/2(10)(7)`.
4. **96**. A cube has 6 faces: `6(4^2)=96`.
5. **B, 12π**. `C=2πr`.
6. **C, 25π**. Radius is `5`; `A=πr^2`.
7. **B, 4π**. Quarter of circumference `16π`.
8. **B, 6π**. `60/360 × 36π = 6π`.
9. **D, 90°**. A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of tangency.
10. **B**. Circle form is `(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2`.
11. **5**. Completing squares gives `(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2=25`.
12. **70°**. Triangle angles sum to `180°`.
13. **100°**. Exterior angle equals sum of remote interior angles.
14. **12**. `8×3/2=12`.
15. **10**. `6^2+8^2=100`.
16. **B, 7√2**.
17. **10**. In `30-60-90`, hypotenuse is twice the shortest side.
18. **3/5 = 0.6**. `sin θ=opposite/hypotenuse=6/10`.
19. **B, cos(55°)**. `sin θ=cos(90°-θ)`.
20. **C, 5π/6**. `150×π/180=5π/6`.

</details>
