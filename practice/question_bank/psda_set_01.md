# Problem-Solving and Data Analysis Question Bank — Set 01

20 оригинальных задач: ratios, rates, percentages, statistics, two-variable data, probability, sampling, margin of error и study design.

---

## Questions

### 1. Easy · SPR · Ratio · Manual
The ratio of boys to girls in a club is `3:5`. There are `40` students total. How many are boys?

### 2. Easy · SPR · Unit rate · Manual
A car travels `180` miles in `3` hours at a constant speed. What is the speed in miles per hour?

### 3. Easy · SPR · Unit conversion · Manual
How many minutes are in `2.5` hours?

### 4. Easy · SPR · Percentage increase · Manual
An item costs `$80`. Its price increases by `15%`. What is the new price, in dollars?

### 5. Medium · SPR · Reverse percentage · Manual
After a `20%` increase, a price is `$120`. What was the original price?

### 6. Medium · SPR · Percent change · Manual
A quantity increases from `50` to `65`. What is the percent increase?

### 7. Easy · SPR · Mean · Manual
What is the mean of `4, 7, 9, 10`?

### 8. Medium · SPR · Missing value from mean · Manual
Five numbers have a mean of `12`. Four of the numbers have a sum of `50`. What is the fifth number?

### 9. Easy · Multiple Choice · Median · Manual
What is the median of `3, 5, 8, 9, 20`?

A) 5  B) 8  C) 9  D) 20

### 10. Medium · SPR · IQR · Manual
For the data set

```text
2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16
```

what is the interquartile range (`IQR`)?

### 11. Medium · Multiple Choice · Standard deviation · Manual
Which data set has the greater standard deviation?

A) `9, 10, 11`  
B) `0, 10, 20`  
C) They have the same standard deviation  
D) Cannot be determined

### 12. Easy · Multiple Choice · Scatterplot association · Manual
As study time increases, test score tends to increase. Which type of association best describes the relationship?

A) Positive  B) Negative  C) None  D) Causal by definition

### 13. Medium · SPR · Linear model · Manual
A line of best fit is

```math
y=2.5x+4.
```

What value does the model predict when `x=8`?

### 14. Medium · SPR · Residual · Manual
For `x=8`, a model predicts `24`, but the observed value is `22`. What is the residual `observed - predicted`?

### 15. Easy · SPR · Probability · Manual
A bag contains `3` red balls and `7` blue balls. One ball is selected at random. What is the probability of selecting a red ball? Enter a decimal or fraction.

### 16. Medium · SPR · Conditional probability · Manual
In a group of `40` students, `24` are female. Of the female students, `10` play soccer. What is

```math
P(\text{soccer}\mid\text{female})?
```

### 17. Medium · Multiple Choice · Sampling · Manual
A school wants to estimate the fraction of all its students who prefer a later start time. Which method is most likely to produce a representative sample?

A) Ask only members of the debate team  
B) Ask the first 100 students entering the cafeteria  
C) Randomly select students from the complete school roster  
D) Post an optional online poll and use whoever responds

### 18. Medium · Multiple Choice · Margin of error · Manual
A poll estimates support at `62%` with a margin of error of `±4` percentage points. Which interval is consistent with the estimate?

A) 54%–58%  
B) 58%–66%  
C) 60%–64%  
D) 62%–68%

### 19. Hard · Multiple Choice · Sample size / margin of error · Manual
All else equal, what generally happens to the margin of error when the random sample size becomes much larger?

A) It increases  
B) It decreases  
C) It always becomes zero  
D) It becomes unrelated to sample size

### 20. Hard · Multiple Choice · Experiment / causation · Manual
Researchers randomly assign participants to receive either a new study method or the usual study method. The new-method group later performs significantly better. Which conclusion is best supported?

A) The study proves the method works for every student everywhere.  
B) Random assignment strengthens a causal conclusion about the treatment for the studied population.  
C) The result shows only correlation because experiments cannot support causation.  
D) The result can automatically be generalized to every population.

<details>
<summary><strong>Answers and brief solutions</strong></summary>

1. **15**. `3/(3+5)=3/8`; `3/8×40=15`.
2. **60**. `180/3=60` mph.
3. **150**. `2.5×60=150`.
4. **92**. `80(1.15)=92`.
5. **100**. `1.20x=120`.
6. **30%**. `(65-50)/50=15/50=0.30`.
7. **7.5**. Sum is 30; `30/4=7.5`.
8. **10**. Total sum must be `5×12=60`; `60-50=10`.
9. **B, 8**.
10. **8**. `Q1=(4+6)/2=5`, `Q3=(12+14)/2=13`; `13-5=8`.
11. **B**. Its values are spread much farther from the mean.
12. **A, positive**. Both variables tend to increase together.
13. **24**. `2.5(8)+4=24`.
14. **-2**. `22-24=-2`.
15. **3/10 = 0.3**.
16. **5/12**. Conditional denominator is the 24 female students: `10/24=5/12`.
17. **C**. Random selection from the full roster best supports representativeness.
18. **B, 58%–66%**. `62±4`.
19. **B**. Larger random samples generally reduce sampling variability and margin of error.
20. **B**. Random assignment supports causal inference; generalization still depends on sampling/population design.
