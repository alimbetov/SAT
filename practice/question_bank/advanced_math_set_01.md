## Questions

### 1. Easy · SPR · Quadratic roots · Manual
What is the larger solution of
```math
x^2-5x+6=0?
```

### 2. Easy · Multiple Choice · Vertex · Manual
What is the vertex of
```math
y=(x-4)^2-7?
```
A) `(-4,-7)`  B) `(4,-7)`  C) `(4,7)`  D) `(-4,7)`

### 3. Hard · SPR · Discriminant / parameter · Manual
For what value of `k` does
```math
x^2+4x+k=0
```
have exactly one real solution?

### 4. Easy · Multiple Choice · Factoring · Manual
Which expression is equivalent to `x^2-9`?

A) `(x-9)(x+1)`  B) `(x-3)^2`  C) `(x-3)(x+3)`  D) `(x+9)(x-1)`

### 5. Medium · Multiple Choice · Equivalent expressions · Manual
For `x\ne0`, which expression is equivalent to
```math
\frac{2x^2+6x}{2x}?
```
A) `x+3`  B) `x+6`  C) `2x+3`  D) `x^2+3`

### 6. Medium · SPR · Factor theorem · Manual
If `(x-2)` is a factor of
```math
P(x)=x^2+kx-6,
```

### 7. Medium · SPR · Remainder theorem · Manual
What is the remainder when
```math
P(x)=x^3-4x+5
```
is divided by `(x-2)`?

### 8. Easy · Multiple Choice · Exponential growth · Manual
A population starts at `500` and grows by `8%` each year. Which model represents the population after `t` years?

A) `500(0.92)^t`  B) `500(1.08)^t`  C) `508^t`  D) `500+8t`

### 9. Easy · Multiple Choice · Exponential decay · Manual
A machine worth `$1200` loses `15%` of its value each year. Which model gives its value after `t` years?

A) `1200(1.15)^t`  B) `1200(0.15)^t`  C) `1200(0.85)^t`  D) `1200-15t`

### 17. Medium · SPR · Line-parabola intersections · Desmos useful


### 18. Hard · Multiple Choice · Quadratic representation · Manual
Which form most directly shows the maximum or minimum value of a quadratic function?

A) `ax^2+bx+c`  B) `a(x-r_1)(x-r_2)`  C) `a(x-h)^2+k`  D) `mx+b`

### 19. Hard · SPR · Parameter / roots · Manual
The quadratic
```math
x^2-6x+k=0
```
has roots that differ by `4`. What is `k`?

### 20. 750–800 · SPR · Rational + quadratic reasoning · Desmos useful
For `x\ne2`, solve
```math
\frac{x^2-4}{x-2}=7.
```

---

<details>
<summary><strong>Answers and brief solutions</strong></summary>

1. **3**. `(x-2)(x-3)=0`.
2. **B, (4,-7)**. Vertex form is `a(x-h)^2+k`.
3. **4**. One real root means `D=0`: `16-4k=0`.
4. **C**. Difference of squares.
5. **A, x+3**. Factor numerator: `2x(x+3)`.
6. **1**. `P(2)=4+2k-6=0`.
7. **5**. Remainder is `P(2)=8-8+5`.
8. **B**. Growth factor is `1+0.08=1.08`.
9. **C**. Remaining factor is `1-0.15=0.85`.
10. **3**. `16=2^4`, so `x+1=4`.
11. **3/2**. `3=6(x-1)`.
12. **C**. Denominator `(x-3)(x+3)` cannot be zero.
13. **11**. Square both sides: `x+5=16`.
14. **4**. Squaring gives candidates `1,4`; only `4` satisfies the original equation.
15. **3**. Solutions are `5` and `-2`.
16. **C, 2**. `x^2=4` gives `x=±2`.
17. **3**. `x^2=2x+3` → `(x-3)(x+1)=0`.
18. **C**. Vertex form displays `(h,k)` directly.
19. **5**. Roots are centered at `3`; if they differ by 4, roots are `1` and `5`, product `k=5`.
20. **5**. For `x≠2`, expression simplifies to `x+2=7`.

</details>
