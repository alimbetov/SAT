### 20. Medium · SPR · Word problem · Manual
A subscription charges a one-time fee of `$18` plus `$5` per month. The total cost was `$63`. How many months was the subscription active?

---

<details>
<summary><strong>Answers and brief solutions</strong></summary>

1. **C, 6**. `3x=18`, so `x=6`.
2. **7**. `3x=21`.
3. **B, 4**. Both sides must be identical: `4(x+2)=4x+8`.
4. **C**. Subtracting `3x` gives `5=-2`, impossible.
5. **2**. `m=(11-3)/(6-2)=8/4=2`.
6. **D, 7**. In `y=mx+b`, `b=7`.
7. **B**. `y-5=4(x-2)`, so `y=4x-3`.
8. **6**. Set `y=0`: `2x=12`.
9. **D, 5/2**. Parallel lines have equal slopes.
10. **C, 3/2**. Perpendicular slope is the negative reciprocal.
11. **5**. Add equations: `2x=10`.
12. **3**. From `x=5-y`; substitute to get `10-2y+3y=13`.
13. **A**. Same slope, different intercepts → parallel distinct lines.
14. **B, x<-3**. `-3x>9`; divide by `-3` and reverse the sign.
15. **7**. `35+12n<=119`; `12n<=84`; `n<=7`.
16. **C, (2,4)**. `4>2(2)-1=3`.
17. **17**. `4(6)-7=17`.
18. **C**. Initial amount `120`, rate `+8` per minute.
19. **4**. Match the coefficient of `x`: `k-1=3`.
20. **9**. `18+5m=63`; `5m=45`; `m=9`.

</details>
