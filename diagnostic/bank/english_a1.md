## EN-A1-02 — Present Simple

**EN-A1-02-Q1**

Every morning Sara ___ to school by bus.

A) go  
B) goes  
C) going  
D) is go

**EN-A1-02-Q2**

My parents ___ coffee in the morning.

A) drinks  
B) is drink  
C) drink  
D) drinking

**EN-A1-02-Q3**

Does he ___ English at home?

A) speak  
B) speaks  
C) speaking  
D) spoke

**EN-A1-02-Q4**

My brother ___ not like spicy food.

A) do  
B) is  
C) does  
D) has

**EN-A1-02-Q5**

Laura works at a hospital. Which question correctly asks for her workplace?

A) Where Laura works?  
B) Where does Laura work?  
C) Where do Laura work?  
D) Where is Laura work?

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — EN-A1-02</summary>

1. <u>**B) goes**</u>. Third-person singular in Present Simple adds -s: Sara goes. A daily routine uses Present Simple.

2. <u>**C) drink**</u>. Plural subject 'parents' takes base verb 'drink' in Present Simple.

3. <u>**A) speak**</u>. After auxiliary 'does', use the bare infinitive 'speak', not 'speaks'.

4. <u>**C) does**</u>. Present Simple negation for 'my brother' is 'does not + base verb'.

5. <u>**B) Where does Laura work?**</u>. Wh-question in Present Simple: Where + does + singular subject + base verb?

</details>

## EN-A1-03 — Articles, plurals and demonstratives

**EN-A1-03-Q1**

I ate ___ apple after lunch.

A) a  
B) an  
C) the one  
D) any

**EN-A1-03-Q2**

One child is outside. Two ___ are inside.

A) childs  
B) childes  
C) children  
D) childrens

**EN-A1-03-Q3**

___ books over there are mine, not these books here.

A) This  
B) That  
C) Those  
D) A

**EN-A1-03-Q4**

She is ___ engineer.

A) a  
B) an  
C) the one  
D) some

**EN-A1-03-Q5**

___ sun rises in the east.

A) A  
B) An  
C) The  
D) Some

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — EN-A1-03</summary>

1. <u>**B) an**</u>. Before the vowel sound in 'apple', use the indefinite article 'an'.

2. <u>**C) children**</u>. 'Child' has irregular plural 'children'.

3. <u>**C) Those**</u>. 'Those' refers to plural items at a distance; 'these' refers to ones nearby.

4. <u>**B) an**</u>. 'Engineer' begins with a vowel sound, so a singular indefinite profession takes 'an'.

5. <u>**C) The**</u>. For the unique sun of Earth in this context, English uses 'the sun'.

</details>

## EN-A1-04 — There is/are and basic prepositions

**EN-A1-04-Q1**

___ a book on the desk.

A) There are  
B) There is  
C) They is  
D) It have

**EN-A1-04-Q2**

___ three chairs in this room.

A) There is  
B) It are  
C) There are  
D) There have

**EN-A1-04-Q3**

The cat is sleeping ___ the table, below its surface.

A) on  
B) under  
C) above  
D) between

**EN-A1-04-Q4**

There ___ any apples left in the basket.

A) isn't  
B) aren't  
C) don't  
D) doesn't

**EN-A1-04-Q5**

Choose the grammatically correct sentence.

A) There is two windows.  
B) There are a window.  
C) There are two windows.  
D) There be two windows.

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — EN-A1-04</summary>

1. <u>**B) There is**</u>. Singular countable noun 'a book' follows 'There is'.

2. <u>**C) There are**</u>. Plural 'three chairs' takes 'There are'.

3. <u>**B) under**</u>. 'Under' means below the table; 'on' would mean on its upper surface.

4. <u>**B) aren't**</u>. Plural 'apples' with 'any' in a negative existential sentence takes 'There aren't'.

5. <u>**C) There are two windows.**</u>. Plural 'two windows' requires 'There are two windows'.

</details>

## EN-A1-05 — Possessives, can and basic questions

**EN-A1-05-Q1**

This notebook belongs to me. It is ___ notebook.

A) my  
B) mine  
C) me  
D) I

**EN-A1-05-Q2**

Those shoes belong to Omar. They are ___.

A) he  
B) him  
C) his  
D) her

**EN-A1-05-Q3**

She ___ swim very well.

A) cans  
B) can  
C) is can  
D) can to

**EN-A1-05-Q4**

Choose the correct question.

A) Can you ride a bicycle?  
B) You can ride a bicycle?  
C) Do can you ride a bicycle?  
D) Can ride you a bicycle?

**EN-A1-05-Q5**

___ your parents live nearby?

A) Does  
B) Are  
C) Do  
D) Is

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — EN-A1-05</summary>

1. <u>**A) my**</u>. A possessive determiner before a noun is 'my'; 'mine' stands without the noun.

2. <u>**C) his**</u>. The possessive pronoun 'his' stands alone: 'They are his'.

3. <u>**B) can**</u>. Modal 'can' has no -s and takes a base verb: 'She can swim'.

4. <u>**A) Can you ride a bicycle?**</u>. A neutral ability question places modal before subject: 'Can you ride...?'

5. <u>**C) Do**</u>. 'Parents' is plural, so Present Simple question uses 'Do ... live?'

</details>
