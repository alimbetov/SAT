## EN-B1-02 — Past Continuous vs Past Simple

**EN-B1-02-Q1**

I was already at the stove preparing dinner. I ___ when the phone suddenly rang.

A) cooked  
B) was cooking  
C) am cooking  
D) have cooked

**EN-B1-02-Q2**

At exactly 7 p.m. yesterday, they ___ football.

A) play  
B) played  
C) were playing  
D) have played

**EN-B1-02-Q3**

While I was walking home, I ___ an old friend.

A) meet  
B) met  
C) was meeting  
D) have met

**EN-B1-02-Q4**

Choose the correct sentence.

A) When the phone rang, she was sleeping.  
B) When the phone was ringing, she sleeping.  
C) When the phone rang, she were sleeping.  
D) When the phone rang, she has slept.

**EN-B1-02-Q5**

We had started preparing for the exam and were still in the middle of it. The lights went out while we ___ for the exam.

A) study  
B) were studying  
C) studied yesterday  
D) have studied

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — EN-B1-02</summary>

1. <u>**B) was cooking**</u>. The first sentence explicitly establishes an action in progress when the phone rang. Past Continuous 'was cooking' describes the ongoing activity; the ringing is the interrupting event.

2. <u>**C) were playing**</u>. Action in progress at a specified past time: 'were playing'.

3. <u>**B) met**</u>. Completed event that occurs during a background action takes Past Simple: 'met'.

4. <u>**A) When the phone rang, she was sleeping.**</u>. Past Simple for the interruption 'rang'; Past Continuous for background 'was sleeping'.

5. <u>**B) were studying**</u>. The context explicitly says the studying was already in progress when the lights went out, so the background action uses Past Continuous 'were studying'.

</details>

## EN-B1-03 — First and second conditionals

**EN-B1-03-Q1**

If it rains tomorrow, we ___ the picnic indoors.

A) move  
B) moved  
C) will move  
D) would moved

**EN-B1-03-Q2**

If I had more free time, I ___ another language.

A) will learn  
B) would learn  
C) learned  
D) have learned

**EN-B1-03-Q3**

Unless you study, you ___ the test.

A) would fail yesterday  
B) will fail  
C) failed yesterday  
D) failing

**EN-B1-03-Q4**

If I ___ you, I would check the instructions twice.

A) am  
B) was being  
C) were  
D) will be

**EN-B1-03-Q5**

If she ___ regularly, she will improve her vocabulary.

A) practice  
B) practices  
C) will practice  
D) practiced

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — EN-B1-03</summary>

1. <u>**C) will move**</u>. Real possible future: If + present, will + base verb.

2. <u>**B) would learn**</u>. Imaginary present condition uses second conditional: If + past, would + verb.

3. <u>**B) will fail**</u>. 'Unless' means 'if not'; likely future result uses 'will fail'.

4. <u>**C) were**</u>. Hypothetical advice uses the conventional form 'If I were you'.

5. <u>**B) practices**</u>. Third-person singular in the first conditional if-clause is 'practices', not 'will practice'.

</details>

## EN-B1-04 — Relative clauses and basic passive

**EN-B1-04-Q1**

The teacher ___ helped us yesterday is very patient.

A) which  
B) who  
C) where  
D) whose

**EN-B1-04-Q2**

This is the book ___ I borrowed from the library.

A) where  
B) whose  
C) which  
D) who

**EN-B1-04-Q3**

The bridge ___ in 2010.

A) built  
B) was built  
C) is build  
D) has build

**EN-B1-04-Q4**

English ___ in many countries.

A) speaks  
B) is spoken  
C) spoke  
D) is speaking by

**EN-B1-04-Q5**

Choose the correct sentence.

A) The letters were sent yesterday.  
B) The letters was sent yesterday.  
C) The letters were send yesterday.  
D) The letters sent by yesterday.

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — EN-B1-04</summary>

1. <u>**B) who**</u>. 'Who' is a subject relative pronoun referring to a person.

2. <u>**C) which**</u>. 'Which' refers to a thing ('the book') in this defining relative clause.

3. <u>**B) was built**</u>. Completed past passive is 'was + past participle': 'was built'.

4. <u>**B) is spoken**</u>. General present passive: 'is spoken'.

5. <u>**A) The letters were sent yesterday.**</u>. Plural subject 'letters' requires 'were + past participle sent'.

</details>

## EN-B1-05 — Reported speech and verb patterns

**EN-B1-05-Q1**

Yesterday Anna said, 'I am tired.' Reported later in the past, Anna said she ___ tired.

A) is  
B) was  
C) be  
D) has

**EN-B1-05-Q2**

He ___ me that the meeting was canceled.

A) said  
B) told  
C) spoke  
D) talked

**EN-B1-05-Q3**

She enjoys ___ books on weekends.

A) read  
B) to read  
C) reading  
D) reads

**EN-B1-05-Q4**

They decided ___ early to avoid traffic.

A) leave  
B) leaving  
C) to leave  
D) left

**EN-B1-05-Q5**

He asked me ___ I was ready for the exam.

A) that  
B) whether  
C) what  
D) whose

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — EN-B1-05</summary>

1. <u>**B) was**</u>. With past reporting verb 'said', present 'am' commonly backshifts to 'was'.

2. <u>**B) told**</u>. Use 'told + person' without 'to'; 'said me' is ungrammatical.

3. <u>**C) reading**</u>. The verb 'enjoy' is followed by a gerund (-ing form).

4. <u>**C) to leave**</u>. 'Decide' is followed by the to-infinitive: 'decided to leave'.

5. <u>**B) whether**</u>. A yes/no indirect question after 'asked' takes 'whether' or 'if'; of the choices, 'whether' is correct.

</details>
