## GEO-01 — Lines, angles and triangles

**GEO-01-Q1**

Two angles form a straight line. If one angle is 110°, what is the other?

A) 60°  
B) 70°  
C) 90°  
D) 110°

**GEO-01-Q2**

Two lines intersect, making vertical opposite angles. One angle is 42°. What is its vertical opposite angle?

A) 42°  
B) 48°  
C) 138°  
D) 180°

**GEO-01-Q3**

A triangle has angles of 50° and 60°. What is its third angle?

A) 60°  
B) 70°  
C) 80°  
D) 110°

**GEO-01-Q4**

An exterior angle of a triangle is 120°. One remote interior angle is 50°. Find the other remote interior angle.

A) 50°  
B) 60°  
C) 70°  
D) 120°

**GEO-01-Q5**

Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One corresponding angle is 75°. What is the matching corresponding angle?

A) 15°  
B) 75°  
C) 105°  
D) 180°

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — GEO-01</summary>

1. <u>**B) 70°**</u>. Supplementary angles sum to 180°, so 180°−110°=70°.

2. <u>**A) 42°**</u>. Vertical opposite angles are equal: both are 42°.

3. <u>**B) 70°**</u>. The angles of any triangle sum to 180°: 180°−50°−60°=70°.

4. <u>**C) 70°**</u>. An exterior angle equals the sum of remote interior angles: 120°=50°+70°.

5. <u>**B) 75°**</u>. Corresponding angles are equal for parallel lines cut by a transversal.

</details>

## GEO-02 — Area, volume and scale

**GEO-02-Q1**

Find the area of a rectangle 8 units long and 5 units wide.

A) 13  
B) 26  
C) 40  
D) 80

**GEO-02-Q2**

Find the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and perpendicular height 7 cm.

A) 17 cm²  
B) 35 cm²  
C) 70 cm²  
D) 140 cm²

**GEO-02-Q3**

What is the area of a circle with radius 3 units?

A) 3π  
B) 6π  
C) 9π  
D) 18π

**GEO-02-Q4**

What is the volume of a rectangular prism of side lengths 2, 3 and 4?

A) 9  
B) 18  
C) 24  
D) 36

**GEO-02-Q5**

A square has side length 2 cm and is enlarged by a linear scale factor of 3. What is the new area?

A) 12 cm²  
B) 18 cm²  
C) 24 cm²  
D) 36 cm²

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — GEO-02</summary>

1. <u>**C) 40**</u>. Area is length×width=8·5=40 square units.

2. <u>**B) 35 cm²**</u>. Triangle area=(base×height)/2=10·7/2=35 cm².

3. <u>**C) 9π**</u>. Area A=πr²=π·3²=9π square units.

4. <u>**C) 24**</u>. Volume=2·3·4=24 cubic units; do not add side lengths.

5. <u>**D) 36 cm²**</u>. New side length=6 cm; area=6²=36 cm². Area scales by 3²=9, not by 3.

</details>

## GEO-03 — Right triangles and trigonometry

**GEO-03-Q1**

A right triangle has legs of length 3 and 4. Find its hypotenuse.

A) 5  
B) 6  
C) 7  
D) 12

**GEO-03-Q2**

For acute angle θ in a right triangle, the opposite side is 6 and hypotenuse 10. What is sin θ?

A) 3/5  
B) 3/4  
C) 5/3  
D) 4/5

**GEO-03-Q3**

For an acute angle θ, the opposite leg is 4 and the adjacent leg is 3. What is tan θ?

A) 3/5  
B) 3/4  
C) 4/3  
D) 5/4

**GEO-03-Q4**

What is sin 30°?

A) 0  
B) 1/2  
C) √2/2  
D) 1

**GEO-03-Q5**

A 10-meter ladder rests against a vertical wall. Its foot is 6 meters from the wall. How high does it reach?

A) 4 m  
B) 6 m  
C) 8 m  
D) 12 m

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — GEO-03</summary>

1. <u>**A) 5**</u>. Pythagorean theorem: c²=3²+4²=25, so c=5.

2. <u>**A) 3/5**</u>. sin θ=opposite/hypotenuse=6/10=3/5.

3. <u>**C) 4/3**</u>. tan θ=opposite/adjacent=4/3.

4. <u>**B) 1/2**</u>. In a 30°–60°–90° triangle, the side opposite 30° is half of the hypotenuse.

5. <u>**C) 8 m**</u>. Right triangle: h²+6²=10² ⇒ h²=64 ⇒ h=8 m.

</details>

## GEO-04 — Circles and arcs

**GEO-04-Q1**

A circle has radius 5 cm. What is its circumference?

A) 5π cm  
B) 10π cm  
C) 25π cm  
D) 50π cm

**GEO-04-Q2**

A circle has diameter 10 cm. What is its area?

A) 10π cm²  
B) 20π cm²  
C) 25π cm²  
D) 100π cm²

**GEO-04-Q3**

A sector has central angle 90° and circle radius 4 cm. What is its area?

A) 2π cm²  
B) 4π cm²  
C) 8π cm²  
D) 16π cm²

**GEO-04-Q4**

If a circle's radius doubles, what happens to its circumference?

A) It halves  
B) It doubles  
C) It triples  
D) It quadruples

**GEO-04-Q5**

Find the length of a 60° arc in a circle of radius 6 cm.

A) π cm  
B) 2π cm  
C) 6π cm  
D) 12π cm

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — GEO-04</summary>

1. <u>**B) 10π cm**</u>. Circumference C=2πr=2π·5=10π cm.

2. <u>**C) 25π cm²**</u>. Radius is half diameter=5 cm; A=π·5²=25π cm².

3. <u>**B) 4π cm²**</u>. A 90° sector is a quarter of the circle: (1/4)π·4²=4π cm².

4. <u>**B) It doubles**</u>. Circumference is 2πr, proportional to radius (area, not circumference, quadruples).

5. <u>**B) 2π cm**</u>. Arc length=(60/360)·2π·6=2π cm.

</details>
