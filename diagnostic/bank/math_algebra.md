## ALG-02 — Linear equations in two variables

**ALG-02-Q1**

Which point satisfies the equation 2x + y = 9?

A) (2, 4)  
B) (2, 5)  
C) (3, 4)  
D) (1, 6)

**ALG-02-Q2**

If y = 3x − 2, what is y when x = 4?

A) 8  
B) 10  
C) 12  
D) 14

**ALG-02-Q3**

A line passes through (1, 3) and (3, 7). Which equation describes it?

A) y = x + 2  
B) y = 3x  
C) y = 2x + 1  
D) y = 2x − 1

**ALG-02-Q4**

Which is an equivalent form of 2x − 3y = 12?

A) y = (2/3)x + 4  
B) y = −(2/3)x − 4  
C) y = (2/3)x − 4  
D) y = 2x − 4

**ALG-02-Q5**

A club charges a $12 registration fee and $5 per visit. Its total cost is y = 12 + 5x. What is y for 9 visits?

A) 45  
B) 52  
C) 57  
D) 108

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — ALG-02</summary>

1. <u>**B) (2, 5)**</u>. Substitute x=2,y=5: 2·2+5=9. For the other points the left side is 8, 10, or 8.

2. <u>**B) 10**</u>. Substitute x=4: y=3·4−2=10.

3. <u>**C) y = 2x + 1**</u>. Slope (7−3)/(3−1)=2. Using (1,3), intercept b=1, so y=2x+1.

4. <u>**C) y = (2/3)x − 4**</u>. −3y=12−2x, hence y=(2/3)x−4. Dividing by a negative reverses both signs.

5. <u>**C) 57**</u>. Fixed fee plus variable cost: 12+5·9=57, not just 5·9.

</details>

## ALG-03 — Linear functions, slope and intercept

**ALG-03-Q1**

What is the slope of the line through (2, 5) and (6, 13)?

A) 1  
B) 2  
C) 4  
D) 8

**ALG-03-Q2**

What is the y-intercept of y = −3x + 7?

A) −3  
B) 0  
C) 3  
D) 7

**ALG-03-Q3**

For f(x) = 4x + 1, what is f(−2)?

A) −9  
B) −7  
C) 7  
D) 9

**ALG-03-Q4**

How are the graphs of y = 2x + 1 and y = 2x − 4 related?

A) They intersect at one point  
B) They are the same line  
C) They are parallel distinct lines  
D) They are perpendicular

**ALG-03-Q5**

A gym charges $10 once plus $4 per class. Which function gives the cost C(n) after n classes?

A) C(n)=14n  
B) C(n)=10n+4  
C) C(n)=10+4n  
D) C(n)=4+10/n

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — ALG-03</summary>

1. <u>**B) 2**</u>. Slope is change in y divided by change in x: (13−5)/(6−2)=8/4=2.

2. <u>**D) 7**</u>. At x=0, y=7, so the y-intercept is 7.

3. <u>**B) −7**</u>. f(−2)=4·(−2)+1=−8+1=−7. Multiply the negative input first; then add the constant term 1.

4. <u>**C) They are parallel distinct lines**</u>. Equal slopes (2) but different y-intercepts give parallel, nonidentical lines.

5. <u>**C) C(n)=10+4n**</u>. Separate fixed fee 10 from cost per class 4n: C(n)=10+4n.

</details>

## ALG-04 — Systems of linear equations

**ALG-04-Q1**

Solve x + y = 8 and x − y = 2.

A) (3,5)  
B) (4,4)  
C) (5,3)  
D) (6,2)

**ALG-04-Q2**

Solve y = 2x and x + y = 12.

A) (3,6)  
B) (4,8)  
C) (6,12)  
D) (8,4)

**ALG-04-Q3**

The system is 3x + y = 11 and x − y = 1. What is x?

A) 2  
B) 3  
C) 4  
D) 5

**ALG-04-Q4**

How many solutions does the system y = 2x + 1 and y = 2x − 4 have?

A) No solutions  
B) Exactly one  
C) Exactly two  
D) Infinitely many

**ALG-04-Q5**

A theater sold 13 tickets: adult tickets cost $8, child tickets $5, and total receipts were $83. How many adult tickets were sold?

A) 4  
B) 5  
C) 6  
D) 7

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — ALG-04</summary>

1. <u>**C) (5,3)**</u>. Add both equations: 2x=10 so x=5, then y=3.

2. <u>**B) (4,8)**</u>. Substitute y=2x into x+y=12: 3x=12, x=4 and y=8.

3. <u>**B) 3**</u>. Add equations: 4x=12, so x=3. Then y=2.

4. <u>**A) No solutions**</u>. Both lines have slope 2 but different intercepts; they never meet.

5. <u>**C) 6**</u>. Let a be adult and c child: a+c=13 and 8a+5c=83. Subtract 5(a+c)=65: 3a=18, so a=6.

</details>

## ALG-05 — Linear inequalities

**ALG-05-Q1**

Solve 3x + 2 < 11.

A) x < 3  
B) x > 3  
C) x < 9  
D) x > 9

**ALG-05-Q2**

Solve −2x ≥ 8.

A) x ≥ −4  
B) x ≤ −4  
C) x ≥ 4  
D) x ≤ 4

**ALG-05-Q3**

Which interval represents x > 2?

A) [2, ∞)  
B) (−∞, 2)  
C) (2, ∞)  
D) (−∞, 2]

**ALG-05-Q4**

Solve 5 − 3x < −1.

A) x < 2  
B) x > 2  
C) x < −2  
D) x > −2

**ALG-05-Q5**

A ticket service adds a $15 fixed fee to $6 per ticket. If the budget is at most $57, what is the maximum number of tickets?

A) 6  
B) 7  
C) 8  
D) 9

<details><summary>Teacher-only answer key and explanations — ALG-05</summary>

1. <u>**A) x < 3**</u>. Subtract 2 and divide by positive 3: x<3.

2. <u>**B) x ≤ −4**</u>. Dividing by −2 flips the inequality: x≤−4.

3. <u>**C) (2, ∞)**</u>. The strict inequality excludes 2, so use an open bracket at 2.

4. <u>**B) x > 2**</u>. Subtract 5: −3x<−6. Divide by −3 and reverse sign: x>2.

5. <u>**B) 7**</u>. 15+6n≤57 ⇒ 6n≤42 ⇒ n≤7. Tickets are whole numbers, so maximum is 7.

</details>
