## A1 / EN-A1-01 — Verb to be, agreement

**Q1 — Recognition**  
I ___ ready for class.

A) is  
B) am  
C) are  
D) be

**Q2 — Agreement**  
My friends ___ in the library now.

A) are  
B) is  
C) am  
D) be

**Q3 — Negation**  
This answer ___ correct; the calculation has an error.

A) isn't  
B) aren't  
C) don't  
D) not

**Q4 — Question form**  
___ the problem difficult?

A) Do  
B) Are  
C) Is  
D) Am

**Q5 — Compound subject**  
My sister and I ___ ready for the exam.

A) am  
B) is  
C) are  
D) be

<details>
<summary><strong>A1 — teacher-only answers and explanations</strong></summary>

1. <u><strong>B) am</strong></u>. С местоимением `I` в настоящем времени используем `am` → `I am ready`. Ошибка `is/are` — неверное согласование.
2. <u><strong>A) are</strong></u>. `My friends` — множественное число, поэтому `are`, а не `is`.
3. <u><strong>A) isn't</strong></u>. С единственным числом `this answer` правильное отрицание `is not/isn't`. `don't` не заменяет связку `be`.
4. <u><strong>C) Is</strong></u>. Общий вопрос со связкой `be`: `Is + subject + adjective?` → `Is the problem difficult?`.
5. <u><strong>C) are</strong></u>. `My sister and I` — составное подлежащее (мы), поэтому `are`.

</details>

## A2 / EN-A2-02 — Past Simple

**Q1 — Irregular verb**  
Yesterday we ___ to the museum.

A) go  
B) went  
C) gone  
D) going

**Q2 — Question after did**  
Did Maya ___ the message?

A) sent  
B) send  
C) sends  
D) sending

**Q3 — Negative past**  
We ___ visit our grandparents last weekend because they were away.

A) didn't  
B) weren't  
C) doesn't  
D) aren't

**Q4 — Finished-time marker**  
I ___ the assignment two hours ago.

A) finish  
B) have finished  
C) finished  
D) finishes

**Q5 — Positive and negative past in context**  
Last summer they ___ a small house, but they ___ there for long.

A) rented / didn't stay  
B) rent / don't stay  
C) have rented / haven't stayed  
D) renting / not stayed

<details>
<summary><strong>A2 — teacher-only answers and explanations</strong></summary>

1. <u><strong>B) went</strong></u>. `Yesterday` указывает на завершённое прошлое; Past Simple глагола `go` — `went`. `gone` — past participle, требует вспомогательного глагола.
2. <u><strong>B) send</strong></u>. После `Did` употребляется базовая форма глагола, а не `sent`: `Did Maya send?`.
3. <u><strong>A) didn't</strong></u>. Отрицание смыслового глагола в Past Simple: `did not + infinitive` → `didn't visit`.
4. <u><strong>C) finished</strong></u>. Маркер `two hours ago` требует Past Simple для законченного действия; `have finished ... ago` обычно недопустимо в стандартном тестовом английском.
5. <u><strong>A) rented / didn't stay</strong></u>. `Last summer` — завершённый период; `rented` в утверждении, `didn't stay` — отрицание Past Simple. Другие варианты нарушают время или структуру отрицания.

</details>

## B1 / EN-B1-01 — Present Perfect vs Past Simple

**Q1 — Experience up to now**  
I ___ that film three times so far.

A) saw  
B) have seen  
C) was seeing  
D) see

**Q2 — Finished period**  
We ___ to Astana in 2024.

A) have traveled  
B) travel  
C) traveled  
D) had traveled

**Q3 — Since + still true**  
She ___ here since 2022 and still enjoys her job.

A) worked  
B) has worked  
C) works  
D) had worked

**Q4 — Past participle**  
Have you ever ___ sushi?

A) eat  
B) ate  
C) eaten  
D) eating

**Q5 — Duration**  
We have lived in this city ___ five years.

A) since  
B) during  
C) for  
D) from

<details>
<summary><strong>B1 — teacher-only answers and explanations</strong></summary>

1. <u><strong>B) have seen</strong></u>. `So far` связывает опыт с периодом до настоящего; используется `have + past participle`.
2. <u><strong>C) traveled</strong></u>. `In 2024` — конкретный законченный период, поэтому Past Simple. Past Perfect `had traveled` потребовал бы дополнительной прошлой точки отсчёта.
3. <u><strong>B) has worked</strong></u>. Действие началось в 2022 году и продолжается; `has worked ... since 2022`.
4. <u><strong>C) eaten</strong></u>. После `have` требуется третья форма глагола: `eat–ate–eaten`.
5. <u><strong>C) for</strong></u>. `For` вводит длительность (`five years`), `since` — точку начала (`since 2021`).

</details>

## B2 / EN-B2-04 — Modals of deduction, possibility and regret

**Q1 — Present impossibility**  
Alex is sitting next to me in Almaty right now. He ___ be in Paris right now.

A) can't  
B) must  
C) might  
D) should

**Q2 — Strong past deduction**  
The exam results are excellent; after months of studying, Mira ___ prepared very thoroughly.

A) can't have  
B) must have  
C) must  
D) shouldn't have

**Q3 — Regret about the past**  
I failed the quiz because I ignored the instructions. I ___ read them more carefully.

A) must have  
B) should have  
C) can have  
D) may

**Q4 — Uncertain past possibility**  
I don't know why the lights are on. Someone ___ have left them on by accident.

A) must  
B) might  
C) can't  
D) should

**Q5 — Past impossibility**  
I saw Sam physically in our office at exactly 10 a.m., so he ___ have been on a flight departing from another city at the same moment.

A) must  
B) might  
C) can't  
D) should

<details>
<summary><strong>B2 — teacher-only answers and explanations</strong></summary>

1. <u><strong>A) can't</strong></u>. Физически невозможное предположение в настоящем: `can't be`. `Must be` означает сильную уверенность в противоположном.
2. <u><strong>B) must have</strong></u>. Сильный логический вывод о прошлом: `must have + past participle` → `must have prepared`.
3. <u><strong>B) should have</strong></u>. Упрёк или сожаление о несделанном действии: `should have + past participle`. `Must have` — вывод о прошлом, не сожаление.
4. <u><strong>B) might</strong></u>. Неуверенная возможность в прошлом: `might have left`. `Must have` выразило бы намного более сильную уверенность, которой в контексте нет.
5. <u><strong>C) can't</strong></u>. Подтверждённое присутствие в офисе исключает одновременно вылетавший самолёт из другого города: `can't have been` — отрицательный вывод о прошлом.

</details>
